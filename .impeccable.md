## Design Context

### Users

People using openGym to plan and track strength training, including lifters following structured
hypertrophy blocks. They plan sessions on phones, tablets, and desktops, then use the active workout
screen in the gym.

### Brand Personality

Practical, direct, and trustworthy. Keep the existing openGym gym-tool feel.

### Aesthetic Direction

Preserve openGym's recognizable navigation, compact workout controls, and support for dark and light
themes. Improve clarity within the existing product rather than rebranding it.

### Design Principles

- Keep active workout information quick to scan and reachable by touch.
- Put the next useful action first; let users open supporting detail when they need it.
- Preserve routine and workout history context when schedules change.
- Keep forms and sheets usable on narrow screens, landscape phones, tablets, and desktops.
- Use existing theme tokens so new controls stay legible in dark and light themes.
